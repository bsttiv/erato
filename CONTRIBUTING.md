# Contributing to Erato

Thank you for your interest in Erato. Short-lived branches and descriptive pull requests are welcome.

## Contributor License Agreement

Before we can merge your pull request, you need to sign our [Contributor License Agreement](CLA.md). The CLA bot will comment on your first pull request with instructions; signing takes a single comment.

Erato is open source under the GNU AGPL-3.0, and its author also runs a commercial hosted version. The CLA lets the author use contributions in that hosted version and offer Erato under other licenses. In return, every contribution accepted into this repository stays available under the AGPL-3.0 or another open source license, and you keep the copyright to your work.
